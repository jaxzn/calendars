How to subscribe

Apple Calendar (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

Tap Subscribe, then confirm. On a Mac you can also use File → New Calendar Subscription… and paste the .ics link; set Auto-refresh to every hour or day. To sync to all your devices, choose iCloud as the location.

Google Calendar

Click Google next to a calendar (on a computer), or in Google Calendar on the web go to Other calendars → + → From URL and paste the .ics link (right-click .ics → Copy link). It then appears on your phone too. Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule, often every 8–24 hours.

Outlook

Outlook.com / Microsoft 365: Calendar → Add calendar → Subscribe from web, paste the .ics link. Classic Outlook for Windows: Add Calendar → From Internet.

Kickoffs are stored in UTC so your calendar shows them in your own time zone. Games whose date or time the NFL hasn't set yet (e.g. Week 18) appear as all-day placeholders until they're scheduled.
